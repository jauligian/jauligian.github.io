# The Legend of Zelda NES Remake

A fully-featured recreation of the classic Legend of Zelda NES game built in C#. Featuring keyboard and gamepad controls, multiple dungeons, diverse enemies, boss battles, an inventory system, and even cooperative gameplay with Navi. Includes special moves, character selection, and faithful recreation of the original game mechanics.
